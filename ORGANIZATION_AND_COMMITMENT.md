# Organization and Commitment

## Organizational Structure
We are dedicated to maintaining a collaborative, transparent, and inclusive organizational structure for SimpleCRM project. Every contributor is a valued member of our community.

## Our Commitment
1. **Quality**: We commit to maintaining high standards of code quality and documentation.
2. **Respect**: We commit to treating all contributors and users with respect.
3. **Openness**: We commit to being open to feedback, new ideas, and constructive criticism.
4. **Consistency**: We commit to delivering regular updates and improvements to the project.

By participating in this SimpleCRM project, you agree to uphold these commitments and contribute to a positive environment for everyone.
